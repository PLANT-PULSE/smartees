About Our School

Our History

Established in 2005, Smartees Montessori School began as a small learning center with just 15 students and 2 teachers. Founded by Mrs. Abigail Mensah, an education enthusiast with a vision for quality early childhood education in Elmina.

In 2010, we moved to our current purpose-built campus at Nippon Junction, featuring modern classrooms, a library, computer lab, and expansive playgrounds. Today, we serve over 200 students from Kindergarten to Class 6, with a team of 25 dedicated staff members.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality education that empowers students to achieve their full potential academically, socially, and emotionally, preparing them for future academic success and lifelong learning.

👁️ Our Vision

To be the leading Montessori institution in the Central Region, recognized for excellence in early childhood education and for developing well-rounded, confident, and responsible individuals.
